The complete AssentTag journey

From your first hello
to your final say.

Create your identity, share a moment, and choose how you appear. Follow every step from registration to consent approval.

01Create your identity

A profile that starts with you.

Enter your details, upload your photo, and capture a live webcam image. AssentTag compares the two faces and checks for duplicate identities before enrolling your profile.

Profile details · Photo upload · Live face capture
02Sign in

Your gateway to a private world.

Sign in with your registered email and password. Your selected access role takes you to the appropriate user or administrator dashboard.

Email & password · Role selection · Secure session
03Find your community

Your people. Your shared moments.

Browse the feed and stories, connect with other people, and reach your profile, messages, uploads, and consent notifications from one dashboard.

Social feed · Stories · Connections
04Share a photo

Post a moment with care.

Choose your photo, add a caption, and select the people you want to tag. Complete the required live identity check before the photo enters the sharing workflow.

Photo & caption · Friend tags · Identity check
05Detect & match

Recognize the people in the picture.

The face engine locates faces and compares their descriptors with enrolled identities. Registered people can then be linked to consent requests for that photo.

Face detection · Landmarks · Identity matching
06Protect first

Privacy before permission.

Faces awaiting consent are obscured in the shared image. The moment stays visible while the person's facial identity stays protected until a decision is made.

Face blurring · Pending consent · Protected sharing
07Notify & verify

Let the person make the decision.

Tagged people receive a request in their notification center. A live face check verifies their identity before they review and respond to the consent request.

Tag notification · Live verification · Review request
08Consent approval

One choice. Only your face revealed.

Approval reveals the consenting person's face. Other pending faces stay protected, and declining a request keeps the privacy blur in place.

Explicit approval · Selective reveal · Privacy retained
